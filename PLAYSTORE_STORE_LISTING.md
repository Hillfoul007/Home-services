## 11. TIPS FOR HIGHER CONVERSION

✅ **DO:**
- Use power words: Quick, Reliable, Professional, Safe
- Include specific benefits: 24-hour, doorstep, transparent
- Show real user testimonials if available
- List all service types clearly
- Highlight what makes you unique
- Include clear CTA: "Download Now"
- Be honest about service areas

❌ **DON'T:**
- Make false claims
- Hide pricing or conditions
- Use misleading screenshots
- Make typos or grammatical errors
- Forget to mention important info
- Oversell or overpromise
- Leave required fields blank
